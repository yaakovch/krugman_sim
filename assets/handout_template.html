First-Generation Balance-of-Payments Crisis

In the Krugman/Flood-Garber model, domestic credit grows under a fixed exchange rate. Reserves fall while the peg is defended, but forward-looking speculators attack before reserves mechanically reach zero, when the shadow floating exchange rate reaches the peg.

Equations

Fixed peg: R(t) = R0 - mu * t, so t0 = R0 / mu.

Shadow float: s_tilde(t) = D0 + mu * t + alpha * (i* + mu).

Attack condition: s_tilde(tc) = sbar. Under a consistent peg, tc = t0 - alpha.
